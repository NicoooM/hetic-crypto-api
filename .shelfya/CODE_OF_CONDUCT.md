# Code of Conduct

All participants in the HETIC Crypto API project are expected to uphold this Code of Conduct. By contributing, you agree to follow these guidelines and help create a welcoming and respectful community.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful and considerate in discussion.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Acknowledge and respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include, but are not limited to:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on any protected characteristic.
- Insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any project participant.
- Use of sexualized language or imagery in public spaces.
- Persistent disruption of discussions or other community activities.
- Publishing others’ private information, such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at [maintainers@hetic-crypto-api.io](mailto:maintainers@hetic-crypto-api.io). Reports should include:

- Description of the incident.
- Date, time, and location (e.g., GitHub issue, pull request, chat room).
- Names or handles of the people involved.
- Any relevant screenshots or logs.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Depending on the severity, consequences may include:

- A private warning from the maintainers.
- Temporary or permanent removal of contributor privileges.
- A public apology or retraction.
- Banning from the repository or community channels.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you witness or experience harassment or inappropriate behavior:

1. Do not respond directly to the offender.
2. Document the incident (screenshots, links, timestamps).
3. Send a private report to [maintainers@hetic-crypto-api.io](mailto:maintainers@hetic-crypto-api.io) or open a confidential GitHub issue titled “Code of Conduct Report”.
4. Maintain confidentiality—do not share details publicly without consent.
